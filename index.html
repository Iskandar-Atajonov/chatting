Core ideas

Four ideas that break intuition

01

Superposition

A system can exist as a blend of several states at once, until a measurement picks one.

02

Entanglement

Two particles can share a single state, so measuring one is correlated with the other at any distance.

03

Wave-particle duality

Light and matter produce interference patterns like waves, yet land as single points like particles.

04

Uncertainty

Position and momentum cannot both be known exactly. The limit is Δx·Δp ≥ ħ/2.

Constants

The numbers behind it

6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s

Planck's constant h, the scale of the quantum world.

299,792,458 m/s

The speed of light in vacuum, exact by definition.

1.055 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s

The reduced Planck constant ħ = h / 2π.

History

From a hack to a theory

1900

Max Planck proposes that energy is exchanged in discrete quanta.

1905

Einstein explains the photoelectric effect with quanta of light.

1925 to 1927

Heisenberg and Schrödinger formulate quantum mechanics; Heisenberg states the uncertainty principle.

1964

John Bell shows entanglement can be tested against local hidden variables.

2022

Aspect, Clauser and Zeilinger win the Nobel Prize for their entanglement experiments.
